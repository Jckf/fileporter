    What's the difference?

    Private passes everything through a relay server, so neither of you sees the other's IP address. The relay only forwards encrypted data and can't read your file. It's slower.

    Fast connects your devices directly when the network allows it. It's much quicker, but you can see each other's IP addresses. It's only used if both of you choose Fast; otherwise the transfer stays private.
